# Research dossier template

Use this structure when saving `posts/<slug>/research_dossier.md`.

## Section 1 — Primary source
What was announced/claimed. What evidence/caveats were given. What a casual reader would overlook.

## Section 2 — Independent analysis
How press/industry coverage frames it. What they emphasize, predict, criticize.

## Section 3 — Community perspective
Reddit/HN/X reactions: excitement, concerns, recurring questions, unexpected use cases, misconceptions. Look for patterns, not individual opinions — this is usually where the real angle is hiding, because press coverage tends to repeat the official framing.

## Section 4 — Cross-source comparison
- Universal agreement
- Where sources disagree
- What only one source mentions
- Blind spots nobody is discussing
- Any contradictions (investigate before continuing)

## Section 5 — Second-order thinking
What changes because of this. Who benefits, who loses. What becomes easier or obsolete. What happens if this trend continues a year out.

## Section 6 — The insight
Complete this sentence: "The real story isn't ___. The real story is ___."

## Section 7 — Mental model
One sentence that frames this as an instance of a broader pattern (e.g. "this is AI shifting from generation to verification").

## Section 8 — Prediction
One evidence-based prediction that follows from the research, not speculation for its own sake.

## Section 9 — Final summary
If you could communicate only one idea from all this research, what is it? That becomes the thread's spine.

## Sources
List every URL actually read, not just cited.

# Editorial review rubric

Score each 1–10 before showing a draft to the user. Anything below 8 → rewrite, don't ship.

| Category | What to check |
|---|---|
| Scroll-stopping hook | Does line 1–2 create immediate curiosity / reveal something surprising? Generic openers ("Most people...", "Here's why...") fail this automatically. |
| Original insight | Could another AI reading only the same one article produce nearly this thread? If yes, score below 6. |
| Human voice | Flag "leverage," "synergy," "paradigm," "game-changing," "powerful," "revolutionary" — replace with concrete language. |
| Specificity | Count abstract statements; each one needs a concrete example, name, date, or exact quote. |
| Memorability | Is there at least one sentence someone would repost standalone? If not, write one. |
| Storytelling | Does each tweet lead into the next, or does it read like a blog post chopped into paragraphs? |
| Emotional impact | Surprised / worried / impressed / curious — neutral-toned threads get rewritten. |
| Discussion potential | Is there a prediction, opinion, or open question someone could disagree with or reply to? |
| Visual synergy | Does the attached image reinforce the specific hook, or is it generic decoration that could be swapped without changing anything? |
| AI-detection | Uniform paragraph lengths, repetitive sentence structure, obvious template scaffolding, over-explaining, "Moreover/Furthermore" transitions — all are tells to remove. |
